## D. Guarantee type: **Conditional**

Freedom 40, the lead guarantee, is **conditional**: it pays out only "if the founder follows the
plan". It is tied to an outcome (40+ hours back in 30 days), and the remedy is a free month, not a refund.

**Why conditional:** it matches the audience filter from Q2 (founders who will delegate). It puts
the risk on Pareto only when the founder does their part. It is also Kasim's real guarantee, so
we don't need a Proposed one. The two supporting guarantees cover the other fears: Matching
(risk before signing) and Lifetime Replacement (unconditional on replacement: "anytime, for any reason").
Source: `02_Offer/right-hand-program-pricing.md`
