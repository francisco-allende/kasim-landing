## 3. Problem

**Objection it fights:** "Do they get me?"

**Headline:** You don't have a people problem. You have a delegation problem.

**Subhead:** Most assistant placements fail for a reason nobody talks about: no one trains the founder.

**Body:**
If you've hired help before, you probably know how it goes.

You hand something off. You check it anyway. You answer the questions it comes back with. You fix
the one thing that slipped. And somehow the work still runs through you.

Someone who gets 9 out of 10 things done isn't 90% useful. You still have to watch all 10,
because you don't know which one they'll miss. By that measure they're worth close to zero.

What you want is someone you never have to monitor. That takes the right person, and it takes you
learning how to hand things off.

**CTA:** none

*Voice device #1 of 2: rhythm through repetition ("You hand… You check… You answer… You fix…").*
